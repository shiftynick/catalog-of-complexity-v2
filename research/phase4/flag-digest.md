- [BLOCKER / citation] spatial_extent / energy_rate_density (sources.iau2015)
  FINDING: The entry cites arXiv:1510.06262 for 'IAU 2015 Resolution B3 on Recommended Nominal Conversion Constants for Selected Solar and Planetary Properties.' Web-verified directly: arXiv:1510.06262 is actually 'IAU 2015 Resolution B2 on Recommended Zero Points for the Absolute and Apparent Bolometric Magnitude Scales' — a different IAU resolution (companion paper, same working group and General Assembly, but different content: B2 defines bolometric-magnitude zero points, not the nominal R☉/L☉/mass-parameter values this entry actually draws on). The correct arXiv ID for Resolution B3 is 1510.07674 (also published as Prša et al. 2016, AJ 152, 41, arXiv:1605.09788). A reader following the cited DOI/arXiv link to verify R☉ = 6.957e8 m would land on the wrong paper.
  SUGGESTED: Correct the iau2015 source entry's arXiv ID from 1510.06262 to 1510.07674 (or cite the AJ-published version, Prša et al. 2016, arXiv:1605.09788). The underlying numeric values (R☉=6.957e8 m, L☉=3.828e26 W) are themselves independently confirmed correct and need no change — only the citation identity is wrong.

- [FIX / plausibility] fractal_dimension_spatial
  FINDING: Entry states the perimeter-area fractal dimension via P ∝ A^(D/2), then reports D=2.15 for large granules. For a 2D projected planar boundary curve under this exact convention, D is mathematically bounded to 1≤D≤2 (D=1 smooth curve, D=2 maximally space-filling curve). D=2.15 exceeds the valid range under the entry's own stated method, an internal contradiction the existing verifier_flags (snippet-verified-only, single-1986-study-not-primary-read) do not specifically call out.
  SUGGESTED: Primary-read Roudier & Muller (1986) to confirm whether D=2.15 is reported under the same P∝A^(D/2) convention stated in the entry, or under a different normalization (e.g. some solar-granulation literature reports a differently-scaled exponent). Either correct the value/convention note or add an explicit caveat flagging the apparent range violation pending primary-read verification.
