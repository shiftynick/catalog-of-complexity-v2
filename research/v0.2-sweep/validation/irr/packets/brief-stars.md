## Scoping note

> The system is the star as a self-gravitating fusion-powered plasma body across
> its main-sequence lifetime, exemplified by the Sun: the interior (core,
> radiative zone, convective zone), the photosphere and its surface-dynamics
> layer (granulation, sunspots, magnetic activity cycle), and the extended
> atmosphere (chromosphere, corona) so far as it is driven by the star's own
> magnetic and convective dynamics. Inside the boundary: the plasma, radiation
> field, and magnetic field participating in hydrostatic/thermal equilibrium
> and in the convective-dynamo system. Outside: the interstellar medium and
> companion bodies (treated as forcing/context, not components), and
> Chaisson's cross-stage evolutionary comparisons (subgiant, red giant, AGB),
> which are cited for context but not scored as this entry's characteristic
> dynamics (this entry scores the mature main-sequence star). Atomicity per
> filling_rules.atomicity-convention: the star is fundamentally a continuum
> plasma/field system with no natural discrete atomic part; the entry follows
> the panel-spec numerosity note and the sweep's own adjudication in treating
> granulation cells (photospheric convective cells, the characteristic
> surface-dynamics unit) as the nearest citable proxy "atom" for numerosity
> and component_ontology purposes, while interaction_topology is correctly
> scored field/continuum rather than particulate. Scoring is class-general
> unless marked exemplar(the-sun); exemplar evidence is necessarily
> Sun-specific since the Sun is the only star with resolved surface dynamics.
