# Citation check — chemical-oscillators

Adversarial web-verification pass on load-bearing citations in
`data/classes/chemical-oscillators.yaml`, cross-checked against the sweep report
(`research/v0.3/expansion/chemical-oscillators-sources.md`).

## Sources checked

### 1. Field, Körös & Noyes 1972, JACS 94(25), 8649-8664
- WebSearch confirms exact title, journal, volume, pages: "Oscillations in
  Chemical Systems. 2. Thorough Analysis of Temporal Oscillation in
  Bromate-Cerium-Malonic Acid System." Found via ACS DOI (pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/ja00780a001)
  and multiple secondary bibliographies.
- **Verdict: PASS** (existence/title/venue) at the level the entry actually
  claims — entry marks this [snippet-verified] and does NOT claim the specific
  period value was pulled from the paper's full text.
- The entry's `characteristic_timescales` batch-period value ("~10-100 s,
  commonly ~30-60 s") is attributed to `sources: [fkn1972]` but the entry's own
  note already discloses "full-text period value not directly pulled this
  sweep." Independent web search (lecture-demo protocols, RSC, CU Boulder) turns
  up a commonly-cited figure of **~20 s** for the standard stirred demonstration
  — inside the entry's broad ~10-100 s envelope, but somewhat below the
  "commonly ~30-60 s" sub-claim, and that ~20 s figure traces to classroom
  protocols, not to FKN 1972 itself. The citation-to-value linkage (attributing
  a specific "commonly ~X s" figure to fkn1972) is not confirmed either way from
  the paper's full text.
  **Verdict: PARTIAL** — value plausible/order-of-magnitude correct but the
  fkn1972 citation is not shown to actually contain the stated period figure;
  already flagged evidence_status: measured-untested by the entry itself, so
  this is a fix, not a new blocker.

### 2. Field & Noyes 1974, J. Chem. Phys. 60(5), 1877-1884 (the Oregonator)
- WebSearch confirms exact title/venue: "Oscillations in chemical systems. IV.
  Limit cycle behavior in a model of a real chemical reaction." AIP Publishing
  DOI page and a freely available full-text PDF located
  (math.us.edu.pl mirror). Abstract confirms: FKN mechanism generalized to a
  5-step, 3-intermediate model (the Oregonator), numerically shown to trace a
  stable closed trajectory (limit cycle) in 3-D phase space — matches the
  entry's characterization in `emergence`, `feedback`, and `tipping_transitions`
  (Hopf bifurcation in Oregonator parameter space).
- **Verdict: PASS** at the [snippet-verified] level the entry claims (title/
  existence/abstract-level content confirmed; entry does not overclaim a
  specific Hopf-bifurcation threshold VALUE from this source, only the
  qualitative mechanism claim, which is supported).

### 3. Simoyi, Wolf & Swinney 1982, PRL 49(4), 245-248
- WebSearch retrieved the APS abstract directly: "Experiments on the
  Belousov-Zhabotinskii reaction in a stirred flow reactor reveal behavior that
  is strikingly similar to that generated by one-dimensional maps with a single
  extremum. ... a period-doubling sequence is observed that leads to a regime
  containing both chaotic and periodic states." This is an exact match to the
  entry's claims in `chaos_sensitivity`, `characteristic_timescales` (CSTR
  period-doubling cascade), and `feedback`.
- **Verdict: PASS.** This is the entry's strongest-supported citation — full
  abstract text independently retrieved and matches the entry's paraphrase
  closely, including the "chaotic and periodic states" / "1-D map" framing.
